# Baldor Specialty Foods — Design System

> Complete spec for replication by another agent (e.g. animadev). Source: Figma design system + brand book + baldorfood.com parity. Namespace: `BaldorDesignSystem_2ef168`. Tokens live in `tokens.css`.

Baldor is a Bronx-based specialty-food distributor. The system is **editorial and confident**: condensed uppercase poster display type, a cream paper surface, deep forest greens, and a lime-green accent. It spans marketing, internal/driver communications, signage, decks, and printed documents.

## 3. Spacing, radius, shadow, motion

**Spacing** (Figma scale): `--s-0`0 · `--s-1`2 · `--s-2`4 · `--s-3`8 · `--s-4`12 · `--s-5`16 · `--s-6`20 · `--s-7`24 · `--s-8`32 · `--s-9`40 · `--s-10`48 · `--s-11`64 · `--s-12`96 (px).

**Radius:** `--r-0`0 · `--r-xs`4 · `--r-sm`8 · `--r-md`12 · `--r-lg`16 · `--r-xl`24 · `--r-pill`999px.

**Shadow:** all tinted `rgba(24,34,48,α)` —
- `--shadow-sm` `0 1px 2px /.06`
- `--shadow-md` `0 4px 12px /.08`
- `--shadow-lg` `0 12px 32px /.12`
- `--shadow-xl` `0 24px 56px /.18`

**Motion:** `--ease-out: cubic-bezier(0.2,0.7,0.2,1)` · `--dur-fast: 140ms` · `--dur: 220ms`. Keep motion subtle and functional; ease-out for entrances.

## 4. Components (visual vocabulary)

- **Buttons** — pill or `--r-sm`; primary = `--green-primary` (print) / `--brand` digital, cream label, `.t-button` (uppercase tracked 13px). Secondary = outline in `--border-strong` on cream. Lime `--green-lime` for high-emphasis CTA with ink label.
- **Chips / tags** — pill, small caps; category colors: gold "Local", purple "Featured", sky "Supply Update". Uppercase `.t-eyebrow`.
- **Cards** — `--bg-elev` (white) or `--cream-panel`, `--r-lg`, `--shadow-md`, `1px --border`. Eyebrow + headline + body rhythm.
- **Alerts / callouts** — safety uses `--alert`/`--red`; boxed with strong ink border for driver comms. Icon + short imperative copy.
- **Eyebrows** — uppercase tracked labels above headlines, `--fg-muted` or lime.

---

## 5. Layout patterns

- **Cream editorial** — cream surface, generous margins, display headline + body columns; the marketing/document default.
- **Forest comms canvas** — full-bleed `--green-forest`, cream/lime text; driver postcards, slides, signage.
- **Poster/signage** — gold (`--gold`) caution field, ink bars, huge condensed display; mirrored copy where read in a truck mirror.
- **Numbered list gold-standard** — forest header + QR, lime numerals, CAPS kickers, closing couplet, oval logo (see `assets/gold-standard/`).
- Grid: 12-col editorial; bento for decks. Max 1–2 bg colors per artifact.

---

## 6. Imagery & voice

- **Imagery** — real product/food and facility photography; full-bleed or framed at `--r-lg`. No SVG illustration of subjects; use placeholders when photos aren't supplied.
- **Voice** — direct, confident, plain. Imperative for instructions ("Chock your wheels"). Bilingual EN/ES common for driver-facing material; other languages added as needed. Tagline: *"Quality you can trust. Service that delivers."*
- **No AI-slop tropes** — no aggressive gradients, no emoji (not in brand), no generic rounded-left-border accent cards.

## 8. Replication checklist for another agent
1. Load `tokens.css` (or copy the `:root` block) before anything else.
2. Use display (Ruder Plakat) uppercase for headlines, Herbik for body — never substitute Inter/Roboto/Arial except as the declared fallback.
3. Cream surface by default; forest green for comms canvases; lime as the one accent.
4. Pull spacing/radius/shadow from the scales — don't invent values.
5. Keep to 1–2 background colors per artifact; reserve red/alert for safety.
6. Match the voice: direct, imperative, bilingual for driver-facing pieces.
